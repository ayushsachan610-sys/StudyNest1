# User Personas: StudyNest

Understanding the diverse learning habits, pain points, and daily workflows of university students is critical to designing a high-empathy, calm study workspace. Below are two primary personas reflecting real student demographics for PM2.

---

## Persona 1: Maya Chen (The High-Load Pre-Med / Tech Undergrad)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Year** | 20 years old, 3rd Year B.Sc. Cognitive Science & Data Analytics |
| **Course Load** | 5 demanding STEM modules + 2 weekly lab reports + undergraduate research |
| **Devices** | MacBook Air, iPad for handwritten notes, Android phone |
| **Tech Comfort** | High, but easily fatigued by complex multi-tool stacks |

### Bio & Daily Reality
Maya is dedicated, ambitious, and frequently overwhelmed. Each week, her professors dump dozens of heavy lecture slide decks (50+ slides each), research papers, and lab instructions onto Canvas. She copies action items into Apple Notes, downloads PDFs into a messy "Downloads" folder, and maintains an unorganized Google Calendar. By Thursday night, she cannot remember which folder contains the supplementary reading for her Friday 9 AM quiz.

### Goals & Motivations
- Keep all files, lecture summaries, and upcoming deadlines for each subject grouped together in one coherent place.
- Rapidly turn dense notes into active recall flashcards before weekend study sessions.
- Sit down to study without spending 20 minutes sorting through tabs and folders just to figure out what to tackle next.

### Key Pain Points
- **Context Fragmentation:** Slides in Drive, homework links in email, exam dates on syllabus PDFs.
- **Cognitive Exhaustion:** Opening 10 browser tabs and feeling anxious before even reading a single page.
- **Passive Studying:** Reading slides repeatedly instead of self-testing, leading to lower retention and test anxiety.

### StudyNest Solution for Maya
- Direct Subject Notebooks where files, Cornell lecture notes, and tasks live under the same subject card.
- A streamlined "Today's Plan" on the dashboard that highlights immediate deadlines and prioritizes today's 3 key tasks.
- Instant flashcard generation from lecture notes so she can review concepts in 5-minute study breaks.

## Persona 2: Marcus Vance (The Non-Traditional Business & PM Student)

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age / Year** | 24 years old, Final Year BBA / Product Management Track |
| **Course Load** | 4 courses, 1 capstone project, part-time campus internship (15 hrs/wk) |
| **Devices** | Windows 11 Laptop, iPhone |
| **Tech Comfort** | Moderate; values simplicity, clean visuals, and zero-friction utility |

### Bio & Daily Reality
Marcus balances academic team deliverables, case study prep, and job applications. He does not have time to set up intricate Notion databases or learn complex markdown relational links. He needs a pragmatic study tool that works straight out of the box with zero configuration. When he has a free 45-minute window between meetings, he wants to open his browser, pick a subject, and execute his next study step without friction.

### Goals & Motivations
- Clear, visual tracking of weekly study progress (actual hours and completed deliverables).
- Quick reference for case study notes, framework diagrams, and presentation deliverables.
- Structured study sessions using focused sprint blocks (Pomodoro) to prevent procrastination.

### Key Pain Points
- **Intimidating Productivity Tools:** Over-engineered tools (Notion, Obsidian) require hours of setup, templating, and maintenance that take time away from actual studying.
- **Lack of Task Clarity:** Forgetting deadline milestones until a peer mentions them in a group chat.
- **Shallow Retention:** Cramming 24 hours before a case exam because study sessions were not spaced out.

### StudyNest Solution for Marcus
- Ready-to-use note templates (*Topic Summary*, *Cornell Notes*, *Questions to Revisit*).
- An Agenda-style Study Planner that clearly displays time estimates and overdue notices.
- Built-in Pomodoro focus timer with ambient soundscapes to anchor dedicated study blocks.
- One-click export of data to back up notes and project resources safely.
